About

I am a student in the Honors College at Rutgers University–⁠New Brunswick, studying computer science and mathematics. My research interests lie at the intersection of AI and economics. In particular, I study how agents learn, reason, and interact in multiagent environments, and how to design the rules of these environments to incentivize desirable behavior.

I have the privilege of being advised by Professor Xintong Wang in the Rutgers EconCS group. I was also a visiting student researcher at Princeton’s Center for Information Technology Policy, advised by Professor Manoel Horta Ribeiro, and was a student researcher at the Rutgers Robot Learning Lab, advised by Professor Abdeslam Boularias.

Research strategy

σ = ½ theory + ½ empirical

I am applying to PhD programs in EconCS. I am interested in a mixture of both theoretical and experimental/empirical work.
