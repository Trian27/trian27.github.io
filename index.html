About

I am a student in the Honors College at Rutgers University–⁠New Brunswick, studying computer science and mathematics. My research interests lie at the intersection of AI and economics. In particular, I study how agents learn, reason, and interact in multi-agent environments, and how to design the rules of these environments to incentivize desirable behavior.

I have the privilege of being advised by Professor Xintong Wang in the Rutgers EconCS group. I was also a visiting student researcher at Princeton’s Humans and Machines Lab, advised by Professor Manoel Horta Ribeiro, and was a student researcher at the Rutgers Robot Learning Lab, advised by Professor Abdeslam Boularias.
